### A. The Three Phases of Karmic Accretion

1. **Phase 1: *Sinbyeong* (Traumatic Fragmentation — Pre-Awakening / Early Escape):**
   * *Status:* Surpassed.
   * Involuntary corporate bio-sculpting, forced cartilage resets, and corporate identity erasure operated as a traumatic spiritual illness (*Sinbyeong*). Personas were experienced as invasive corporate parasites threatening to extinguish the Daleketnon birth substrate.
2. **Phase 2: The Living Mosaic (*Wonhan-puri* & The Echo Phenomenon — Active SRM Baseline):**
   * *Status:* Active Operating Reality (Arc 1 Climax &rarr; Arc 2).
   * Recognition that past personas do not die when covers are retired or compromised. Each persona leaves an indelible neuro-somatic imprint: Tanaka Ryo's institutional discipline, Lee Ji-yoo's acoustic poise and micro-expression appraisal, Mei Jing's black-market street math, Zhang Wei's statistical database invisibility and calm elder authority, and Leung Hoi-ching's working-class maritime logistics and grit. Velvet resolves their collective grief (*Wonhan-puri*) by turning their capabilities toward shadow liberation.
3. **Phase 3: The Sovereign *Mansin* (*Upāya* & *Kagami* — Late Arc 2 &rarr; Arc 3):**
   * *Status:* Horizon Target.
   * Full realization of *Anattā* (Non-Self). The un-sculpted baseline is recognized as a quiet sanctuary (*Bin Geureut*), and all personas become *Upāya* (Skillful Means)—deliberate *Nirmāṇakāya* (transformation bodies) deployed to protect the vulnerable and dismantle corporate tyranny without existential friction.

### B. Karmic Indelibility & Trans-Sprawl Re-Emanation of Past Covers

A fundamental tenet of the **Path of the Thousand Lives** is that **karmic learning is permanent and indestructible**:

* **Mundane Burning vs. Karmic Preservation**: When an operational cover is compromised or scrubbed mid-run (such as the burning of *Zhang Wei* during CMP 2083-05), only the mundane legal identity (Fake SIN, port clearance ID, local paper trail) is lost. The neuro-somatic memory, technical proficiency, and lived wisdom acquired during that life remain permanently woven into Velvet's *Bin Geureut* (Empty Vessel).
* **The Living Mosaic of Past Lives (*Jātaka*)**: Inactive or compromised covers do not die; they rest within the quiet center of the vessel as accessible neuro-somatic echoes.
* **Trans-Sprawl Re-Emanation (*Upāya*)**: While Velvet's philosophical relationship with "re-living past lives" continues to develop, the metaphysical framework preserves the capability to re-emanate established personas in distant sprawls (e.g., Europort, Vancouver, Neo-Tokyo) or at future dates under refreshed biometric/legal identities—treating past personas not as disposable lies, but as enduring instruments of Skillful Means.
