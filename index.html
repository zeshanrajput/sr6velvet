[CREATIVE VISION: THE THOUSAND LIVES & THE SACRED MIRROR]

Born a Dalakitnon to a destitute family in the Manila slums, Kim Jin-Young’s rare genetic architecture and extreme natural Charisma made him a prime commodity for Mitsuhama. Bio-sculpted into a pan-Asian idol and deployed as the center Face of TXM (The Death Messengers), his voice and synchronized movement were used to channel immense collective geomantic rituals across metroplexes. Sabotaging a lethal mega-ritual in Singapore to save Hana and shatter the ritual matrix, he vanished into the Seattle shadows to forge sovereign autonomy.

This anthology explores the profound, visceral spaces between tabletop mechanics and literary cyberpunk:

  • The Somatic Tax of Cosmetic Control (R2): Physical transformation is never effortless morphing. It carries a heavy visceral cost—bone cartilage resetting with wet clicks, metallic heat behind the jaw, shortened or broadened ribcages, and localized DNA re-keying beneath every sculpted mask (Lee Ji-yoo, Tanaka Ryo, Mei Jing) vs. the un-sculpted obsidian baseline of his birth heritage.
  • Charisma 10 (14) Engineered Gravity & The Discovery of Empathy: Charisma 10 (14 with buff spells) is an uncanny, suffocating psychological pressure—the peak of corporate bio-engineering. Velvet’s tragedy is the inability to know if anyone’s affection, trust, or pity is authentic, or merely their nervous system collapsing into his manufactured gravity. Over the campaign, Velvet evolves from an algorithmic corporate weapon into someone who discovers genuine empathy by crafting un-monetized sanctuaries (pork broth in West Seattle, roasted Tieguanyin tea in Tacoma) for broken people.
  • The Path of the Thousand Lives (Sinbyeong \(\rightarrow\) Living Mosaic \(\rightarrow\) Upāya): Forced corporate disguise evolves into an artificial reincarnation cycle. Velvet retains indelible neuro-somatic skills, virtues, and grief (Han) from every lived persona, moving from traumatic fragmentation to becoming a Mansin (master of ten thousand spirits) who wears identities as deliberate skillful means (Upāya) to protect the vulnerable.

Arc Progression Breakdown

  1. Story Arc 1: Manufactured Solace & The Honeytrap: Establishing baseline survival in Seattle using weaponized charm; discovering empathy in un-monetized sanctuaries; culminating in the devastating reveal that Hana in Neo-Seoul is bait in a joint Mitsuhama/Wuxing trap.
  2. Story Arc 2: The Sovereign Underground & The Push for Power: Plunging into Seattle’s subterranean depths (Ork Underground, geothermal conduits); forging mutual deterrence pacts (Ni Ni Xiaolu & Octagon Triad, Cutters, Ancients); integrating the Living Mosaic; advancing initiation (Power Points / Linguistics, Empathy, Centering).
  3. Story Arc 3: The Pacific Reckoning & Sovereign Emanation: Transnational counter-offensive across Hong Kong and Neo-Seoul; invoking Great Form Kami; shattering the idol geomantic grid; liberating Hana; establishing permanent sovereign Awakened sanctuaries.

Brief Background & Alignments

Character Goals

  1. Sanctuary & Sovereign Autonomy: Build an unassailable shadow network and subterranean magical sanctuary to protect Awakened refugees from corporate reclamation.
  2. Liberation & Solidarity: Sever Mitsuhama’s ownership claims and liberate Hana from Wuxing’s gilded cage.
  3. Spiritual Mastery: Advance Shinto-Musok initiation (Channeling, Invocation, Great Forms) to channel ancestral spirit ribbons and great kami without succumbing to biological drain.

Secrets

  1. Velvet conceals that his original identity was the center star of Mitsuhama’s TXM. If revealed, MCT Asset Recovery units would deploy kill-teams to reclaim their multi-million nuyen asset.
  2. Velvet secretly channels sensory anchors and shadow resources across the Pacific to support Hana while evading dual-megacorp surveillance.
