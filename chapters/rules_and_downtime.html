Downtime Protocols & Common Rules

This section aggregates core cheat sheets and templates for downtime registration, magic spellcasting, conjuring, and foci benefits.

SRM Monthly Expenses & Downtime Cadence Protocol

Mandatory Campaign Protocol: Every second official Shadowrun Missions (SRM) run (excluding CMPs or non-SRM special events), Velvet must settle monthly expenses and execute mandatory downtime allocations.

1. Monthly Expenses Package

Every 2nd SRM mission, the following recurring obligations are deducted from liquid Nuyen:

  • Lifestyle Expenses: Low Lifestyle (¥2,000/mo) or Middle Lifestyle (¥5,000/mo).
  • Hooder Quality (Rating 3): ¥3,000/mo obligation allocated to community assistance.
  • Coven Dues: ¥2,400/mo dues paid to the Guild of Freelance Assets.

2. Mandatory Downtime Actions (Expense Months)

During an expense month, Velvet receives 1 Major Downtime Action and 1 Minor Downtime Action, allocated as follows:

  • 1 Major Downtime Action: Dedicated to Coven service, ritual maintenance, and keeping Coven Loyalty in good standing.
  • 1 Minor Downtime Action: Working for the People—funded directly by the Hooder ¥3,000 nuyen obligation, generating +1 Karma at no additional nuyen charge.

Sustained Attribute Spell Strategy (Focused Concentration R3)

Velvet’s primary source of attribute augmentations is the Increase Attribute Health spell. Under Focused Concentration (Rating 3), Velvet can sustain up to 3 Increase Attribute spells simultaneously with 0 sustaining penalties, reaching the maximum +4 SRMG Augmentation Cap across three key attributes:

  1. Universal Sustained Anchor 1 — Charisma (+4): Boosts Charisma from 10 to 14 (Exceptional natural/metagenetic baseline + 4).
  2. Universal Sustained Anchor 2 — Willpower (+4): Boosts Willpower from 5 to 9.
  3. Scene-Calibrated Sustained Anchor 3:
    • Social & Legwork Scenes: Boosts Intuition (+4) from 3 to 7 (Unlocks 16d6 Judge Intentions, 10d6 Cosmetic Control shifts, and 12d6 Memory).
    • Combat Scenes (Reflexes/Initiative): Boosts Reaction (+4) from 2 to 6 (Unlocks 9d6 Defense Test and 9 + 1D6 Physical Initiative).
    • Combat Scenes (Hardened Armor/Soak): Boosts Body (+4) from 2 to 6 (Unlocks 7d6 Soak and expands Physical Monitor to 11 boxes).
