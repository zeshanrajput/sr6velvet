Character Creation (Life Path)

NoteArchived Build Draft

This page documents an experimental Life Path draft for historical comparison. The official character build uses the Point Buy Build.

The character was created using the Life Path rules in 6WC.

Born This Way

  • Starting Karma: Karma = 50
  • Metatype: Elf
  • Metavariant: Dalekitnon Karma -= 5
  • Starting Attributes
    • Any attributes for which your metatype has an increased maximum (higher than six) start at 2.
    • All other attributes start at 1.
    • Dalekitnon have increased maximums for Agility, Logic, Intuition, and Charisma
  • Attribute Values
    • Agility = 2
    • Charisma = 2
    • Body = 1
    • Reaction = 1
    • Strength = 1
    • Willpower = 1
    • Logic = 2
    • Intuition = 2
    • Edge = 1
  • If you’re a full magician, mystic adept, or adept, gain Magic = 1
  • Metagenic Qualities
    • None allowed per SRMg p.54
  • Awakened: Mystic Adept
  • Nationality: Philippines
  • Native Language: English
  • Qualities
    • Exceptional Attribute - CHA: Karma -= 12
    • Injury Prone (R3): Karma += 15

Growing Up, Early Childhood, and Adolescence

  • Four Childhood Skills
    • Athletics = 2
    • Con = 2
    • Influence = 2
    • Perception = 2
  • Qualities
    • Hooder (R3): Karma += 15
    • Inspire Competence: Karma -= 11
  • Knowledge: East Asian Pop Culture

Coming of Age: Early Adult and the Transition to Adulthood

  • Choose one skill as your strongest talent
    • Sorcery: Sorcery = 4
  • Define your character’s best attribute (cannot be Edge, Magic, or Resonance)
    • Charisma: Charisma += 5
  • Qualities
    • Chronic Pain: Karma += 15
  • Childhood Contact: 4 Points: Contact_Points += 4, Nuyen += 25000

  • Choose one: +1 to any skill: Sorcery += 1

Traveler

  • Choose one: +1 to any physical attribute: Body += 1
  • Choose one: +1 to any mental attribute: Willpower += 1
