# Downtime Protocols & Common Rules

This section aggregates core cheat sheets and templates for downtime registration, magic spellcasting, conjuring, and foci benefits.

## Spellcasting & Foci Integration

### **Tradition: (Shinto/Musok — Willpower + Charisma)**

* **Starting Attributes**: Willpower 5, Charisma 10, Intuition 4, Body 2.
* **Starting Spellcasting Pool**: Sorcery (6) + Spellcasting spec. (2) + Impr. Ability (Sorc, 4) + Magic (6) + Power Focus (2) = 20
* **Spellcasting Pool**: 20 dice $\rightarrow$ **Buys 5 Hits** (guarantees +4 on all attribute spells).
* **Drain Resistance Pool**: 15 dice (5 WIL + 10 CHA) $\rightarrow$ **Buys 3 Hits** `[SR6, Page 36]`.

### Quickening Attribute Spells

:::{.callout-note}

This represents the **maximum** that Velvet *might* be interested in quickening. It does NOT mean that Velvet has quickened all of these spells.

:::

#### **Step 1: Cast "Temporary Increase Attribute (Willpower)"**

* **Casting**: 20 dice $\rightarrow$ Buys 5 hits. Only take 1 hit to start.
* **Drain (3)**: Resisted with 15 starting drain dice (Buys 3 hits).
* **Drain Taken**: $3 - 3 = \mathbf{0 \text{ Drain}}$.
* *Stat Update*: Willpower is now 6. Drain Pool increases to **16 dice** (6 WIL + 10 CHA).

#### **Step 2: Cast "Increase Attribute (Charisma)"**

* **Casting**: 18 dice (sustaining temporary willpower spell) $\rightarrow$ Buys 4 hits. Charisma is boosted by its maximum of +4 (from 10 to 14).
* **Drain (6)**: Resisted with 16 drain dice (Buys 4 hits).
* **Drain Taken**: $6 - 4 = \mathbf{2 \text{ Drain}}$.
* *Stat Update*: Charisma is now 14. Drain Pool increases to **20 dice** (6 WIL + 14 CHA).
* Total Drain: 2S (no penalty)

#### **Step 3: Cast "Increase Attribute (Willpower)"**

* **Casting**: 18 (sustaining temporary willpower spell) dice $\rightarrow$ Buys 4 hits. Willpower is boosted by its maximum of +4 (from 5 to 9).
* **Drain (6)**: Resisted with 20 drain dice (Buys 5 hits).
* **Drain Taken**: $6 - 5 = \mathbf{1 \text{ Drain}}$.
* *Stat Update*: Willpower is now 9. Drain Pool increases to **23 dice** (9 WIL + 14 CHA).
* Total Drain: 3S (-1 die pools)

#### **Step 4: Cast "Increase Attribute (Intuition)"**

* **Casting**: 19 dice $\rightarrow$ Buys 4 hits. Intuition is boosted by its maximum of +4 (from 4 to 8).
* **Drain (6)**: Resisted with 23 drain dice (Buys 5 hits).
* **Drain Taken**: $6 - 5 = \mathbf{1 \text{ Drain}}$.
* *Stat Update*: Intuition is now 8. (Drain Pool remains 23 dice).
* Total Drain: 4S (-1 die pools)

#### **Step 5: Cast "Increase Attribute (Body)"**

* **Casting**: 19 dice $\rightarrow$ Buys 4 hits. Body is boosted by its maximum of +4 (from 2 to 6).
* **Drain (6)**: Resisted with 23 drain dice (Buys 5 hits).
* **Drain Taken**: $6 - 5 = \mathbf{1 \text{ Drain}}$.
* *Stat Update*: Body is now 6.
* Total Drain: 5S (-1 die pools)

#### **Step 6: Cast "Increase Reflexes"**

* **Casting**: 19 dice $\rightarrow$ Buys 4 hits. Reaction is boosted by +4 (from 4 to 8), and you gain +4D6 Initiative.
* **Drain (8)**: Resisted with 23 drain dice (Buys 5 hits).
* **Drain Taken**: $8 - 5 = \mathbf{3 \text{ Drain}}$.
* *Stat Update*: Reaction is now 8. Init: 16 + 5D6.
* Total Drain: 8S (-2 die pools)

#### **Step 7: Cast "Charm"**

* **Casting**: 18 dice $\rightarrow$ Buys 4 hits.
* **Drain (5)**: Resisted with 23 drain dice (Buys 5 hits).
* **Drain Taken**: $5 - 5 = \mathbf{0 \text{ Drain}}$.

#### **Step 8: Recovery from Drain**

* **Drain Taken**: 8 Stun
* **Recovery**: BOD (6) + WIL (9) = 15 dice = 3 bought hits per hour of rest

## Influence Tests

  * Social Rating: CHA (14) + Tres Chic (12) + Charm (4)= 30 (+1 Situational Edge if Opp. SR is < 26)

### Commanding Voice

SW p. 78

* **Action**: Major Action.
* **Pool**: Influence (5) + Charisma (10 + 4) + Charm (4) + Commanding Voice (1) = **24 dice**
* **Opposed By**: Willpower + Intuition
* **Effect**: Target obeys a single command of 5 words or less, or freezes for 1 round/level.
* **Constraint**: Orders cannot cause direct self-harm.

### Sharp Tongue

SO p. 111

* **Action**: Major Action.
* **Pool**: Charisma (10 + 4) + Magic (6) + Power Focus (2) = **22 dice**
* **Opposed By**: Willpower + Intuition
* **Effect**: Inflict net hits as unresisted Stun damage.
* **Alternate Effect**: Choose to daze before rolling. Succeeds if net hits $\ge$ target's WIL.
* **Constraint**: Target must be in line of sight and understand the adept.

### Inspire Confidence

6WC p. 133

* **Pool:** Charisma (10 + 4) + Influence (6) + Leadership (2) + Charm (4) = **26 dice**
* **Effect**
  * Hits function as a teamwork test for any skill.
  * One character may gain one (1) Edge.
