Character Creation (Point Buy)

This page tracks the character creation process using the Point Buy system (Sixth World Companion).

Shadowrunners are built with 100 Character Points (CP). You still gain 50 points of customization Karma.

  • Character_Points = 100
  • Karma = 50

Choose Your Metatype/Metavariant

  • Choose the metatype or metavariant you want to play: Dalekitnon Karma -= 5
  • Any metagenetic qualities must also be paid for.
    • Metagenetic Attribute Increase (CHA): Karma -= 15

Choose Mundane, Awakened, or Emerged

  • Awakened: Character_Points -= 10
  • Mystic Adepts start with Magic += 1.
    • This unlocks the ability to purchase adept power points (8 CP each for mystic adepts).
      • 3 Adept Power Points: Character_Points -= 24
    • Mystic adepts may purchase a number of spells equal to final Magic x2 - PP. Each spell costs 2 CP or 5 Karma each.
      • Increase Attributes: Karma -= 5

Purchase Special / Metatype Adjustment Points with CP

  • All characters start with 1 Edge: Edge += 1
  • All characters start with 1 metatype adjustment point for free: Magic += 1
  • You may purchase up to 12 additional adjustment points at 4 CP each
    • Magic += 4 : Character_Points -= 16
    • Edge += 1 : Character_Points -= 4

Purchase Attributes with CP

  • All character attributes start at the minimum value for their race.
    • Body = 1
    • Agility = 1
    • Reaction = 1
    • Strength = 1
    • Willpower = 1
    • Logic = 1
    • Intuition = 1
    • Charisma = 1
  • All characters start with 4 attribute points.
    • Charisma += 4
  • You may purchase up to 20 additional attribute points for 2 CP each.
    • Body += 1 : Character_Points -= 2
    • Agility += 2 : Character_Points -= 4
    • Reaction += 1 : Character_Points -= 2
    • Strength += 1 : Character_Points -= 2
    • Willpower += 4 : Character_Points -= 8
    • Logic += 2 : Character_Points -= 4
    • Intuition += 2 : Character_Points -= 4
    • Charisma += 5 : Character_Points -= 10

Purchase Skills with CP

  • All characters start with 12 skill points.
    • Influence += 5
    • Sorcery += 6
    • Conjuring += 1
  • You may purchase up to 20 additional skill points for 2 CP each.
    • Conjuring += 4 : Character_Points -= 8

Purchase Resources with CP

  • All characters start with Nuyen = 10000
  • You may purchase resources at a rate of 1 CP per 20,000 nuyen
    • Nuyen += 40000 : Character_Points -= 2
