Through their contacts they were able to secure an appointment with the person in charge of the local Ancients holdout, and after a round of drone dog fight betting he told them that their target was here to see them before heading to the rival Cutters gang. Bookie offered them money if they’d come across intel on the Cutters. Following the lead, the team went to the Cutters headquarter upstairs the Oshki Piroshki and convinced the person in charge, Trudy, to help them on their quest in exchange for their promise to letting the blame fall on her bosses so she could hopefully rise up the ranks. Trudy pointed the team to a Vory safehouse in Tukwila, and after seeing the goons in the basement, they landed on the roof and found the target in the right room, but it proved to be a trap. After taking care of the goons, they decided to drop their target over the Night Market.

CMP 2083-01 | Nonplushed None

  • Date: 2026-Aug-22
  • GM: CDT Agent Kira Capalu (#TBD)
  • Player: Velvet (WarDoctor)
  • Team: Bast (Bast), Mamba (Dreamwalker), Ninja (ArrogantNinja), Velvet (WarDoctor), Yankee Doodle (Inirlan)
  • Difficulty: Veteran
  • Summary: While being in Amsterdam for one reason or another, the team got hired by two Johnsons – first to find some kidnapped trucks, then to look for clues why it happened. They headed to the last known location, passed the traffic jams via a flying car and found a gas station nearby, where they got in contact with two teenage girls and learned that the crime community of Ghent might be involved. So they brought them to their soccer game and heard of a warehouse shared between three factions, with one of them rumoured to be the perpetrators. With that they went to the double warehouse, where they scouted it astrally and went for the host, only to get surprised by a databomb and finding the system going into alarm mode. Fearing for their traces, the team acted quickly and attacked the warehouse with open firepower. Despite a team of mercenaries that waited in a container for a shipment joining in, the criminals in the warehouse proved no match, and after breaking the resistance, the team interrogated or mindprobed some survivors to find out that it was a paid gig with insider knowledge. While escorting the trucks to their destination in Europort proved uneventful, Simon Andrews called them a second time, revealing to them that their original Johnson was found dead, that they were paid by the same shell company as their opposition, and that he has a bad feeling about this.
